# Brand Research — AryaTormoz Brake Factory (04)

Sector reference study for the visual identity of this demo. We study how the
category signals credibility, then design something original. No third-party
logo, wordmark, or proprietary asset is reproduced.

## Companies studied

| Company | URL | What the identity does |
|---|---|---|
| Brembo | https://www.brembo.com | Iconic engineering red against graphite and machined metal; hero product photography of rotors and calipers; circular rotor geometry recurs throughout the visual language. |
| Akebono | https://www.akebono-brake.com | Technical friction-materials manufacturer; restrained industrial presentation focused on materials science and testing credibility. |

## Category patterns observed

- **Red is the sector's engineering signal**, not a racing gimmick. It appears as a precise accent against graphite, never as a large flooded field.
- **Graphite and machined-metal neutrals** carry the surfaces. The neutral is genuinely cool grey, not the warm brown-grey common in generic dark UI.
- **Circular geometry is the product language** — rotor rings, vent slots, pad arcs, annular segments.
- Credibility comes from **product and process photography**, plus test and tolerance data. This is a manufacturing brand, not a gaming brand.

## What we use

- **Engineering red primary (`#D7352A` / bright `#F04A3C`)** — the strongest industrial statement in the set, used as a precise accent on a cool graphite field.
- **Cool graphite neutrals (`#101214` page, `#879099` steel)** — the steel tone occupies the secondary accent slot, matching the machined-metal convention.
- **Rotor-and-pad mark** — a rotor annulus flanked by an opposing pair of pad arcs, i.e. the friction couple that is literally the product.
- **Amber warning (`#EAB308`)** kept firmly separate from brand red so that a genuine alert can never be mistaken for brand chrome.

## What we deliberately did not copy

- **No racing-game UI**: no carbon-fibre texture, no speed streaks, no chequered flag, no neon glow.
- No caliper silhouette or disc rendering in the Brembo product-photography style, and no imitation of its wordmark.
- No reuse of a studied manufacturer's exact red; ours is a distinct value chosen to hold contrast on both a graphite and a white field.

## Semantic separation note

Because the brand colour is red, the status ramp is explicitly pulled apart:

- **Brand** `#D7352A` / `#F04A3C` — identity only.
- **Danger** `#F2685C` dark, `#C0392B` light — softer and pinker than the brand red so alerts stay distinguishable.
- **Warning** `#EAB308` — amber, unmistakably not the brand.
